# Pricing model

## Destination

A packaging decision someone can be charged under: which framing nagara sells, what it costs, and what a user gets for it. Compute cost per article is already settled at roughly $0.008 (see [[tts-service]]), so this is entirely about what sits on top of that number.

## Bearings

**The ground.** What a user is offered and charged, and nothing about how the system produces an article. The per-article cost is an input here, not a question.

**Read first.** [[tts-service]] for the settled cost; [[validate-demand]], which this waits on and overlaps.

**Standing preferences.** **Nothing here is answerable without an audience to price for.** [[validate-demand]] says so from the other side, listing pricing in its own out-of-scope section, and that adventure carries a trial on which framing reads better. Expect the first real evidence for this adventure to arrive as a by-product of that one, and take it from there rather than re-running it.

## Trials

- **Which framing to sell**, a flat bundle of items against metered audio-minutes. [[validate-demand]] carries the same question as one of its trials, from the reading-better angle rather than the packaging angle.
- **What the price actually is**, which needs a framing first and then a margin someone has agreed to.

Not yet enumerated further, and deliberately so: the ground moves once there is an audience.

## Solved

Nothing yet.

## Out of scope

- **Billing mechanics.** Which processor, what the invoice looks like, how a quota is enforced. Those follow a decision this adventure has not made yet.
