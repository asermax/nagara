# Read-along visual design

## Objective

[[read-along-player-shape]] settled the player's *shape* (layout, navigation, the calm-reader-plus-focus-mode structure) against a deliberately placeholder aesthetic that carried no verdict weight. Develop the *proper* visual design for the player, and by extension the web surface: design language, typography, colour, motion, and light/dark, with the settled shape as the fixed substrate it must not disturb.

## Unknowns

Not yet enumerated as something a single experiment could clear. Judged on aesthetic coherence and quality against real content, once a design direction exists to judge.
