# Quote voice switching

## Objective

Detect quoted passages in an article and switch the Kokoro voice for them, the way an audiobook narrator differentiates dialogue from narration: a listener hears a quote as a distinct voice rather than the narrator reading it flat.

## Unknowns

Not yet enumerated as something a single experiment could clear. Candidates already named: how good attribution and nesting detection can be made without over-triggering on ordinary emphasis or a blockquote that isn't dialogue; whether the read-along highlight should signal the voice switch visually as well as audibly.
